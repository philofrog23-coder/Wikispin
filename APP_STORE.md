## Name and subtitle

- **Subtitle (30 max):** Random articles, one spin away

## Promotional text (170 max)

Pick a topic, spin the reel and read something you'd never have searched for. Writers from 1800s France, obscure orchids, forgotten battles: one tap each.

## Description

WikiSpin turns Wikipedia into a game of chance. Choose a topic, set how deep you want to go, and spin. The reel ticks, slows down and lands on an article you've probably never heard of. Read it, save it, or spin again.

PICK YOUR TOPIC
People, Plants, Sports, Wars, or Anything at all. Go narrower with political leaders, writers, scientists, painters, athletes, battles, trees, cacti and more.

FILTER LIKE A CURATOR
Choose a country, a century, and a spot on the Famous to Obscure slider. Want 19th-century Japanese writers nobody talks about? Two taps.

A SPIN WORTH WAITING FOR
Every spin is a short show: a fast reel, ticking sounds, haptic taps and a chime when it lands. Tap the reel if you'd rather skip straight to the result.

KEEP WHAT YOU FIND
Save articles with a heart, look back through your history, and share any discovery as a card. A daily streak keeps count of everything you've uncovered.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your saved articles and history stay on your device.

Article text comes from Wikipedia and is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Topic lists are built from Wikidata. WikiSpin is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

- **Data collection:** Data Not Collected.
- Saved articles, history and settings are stored only on the device. The app requests article summaries from Wikipedia without identifiers; Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
- Tracking: No.

## Age rating questionnaire

- Cartoon or fantasy violence, realistic violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling, horror, medical information: **None** as app content.
- **Unrestricted web access: Yes.** The reader opens full Wikipedia pages in an in-app browser, and random articles can cover any subject (including wars and mature topics). Answer honestly; Apple usually assigns its highest age rating to apps with unrestricted web access, which is normal for reference apps.
- Contests: No. User-generated content: No. Messaging or chat: No. Advertising: No.

## Review notes

The app has no login. Spins use bundled topic lists plus the public Wikipedia REST API. To test a filtered spin: Spin tab → People → Filters → Writers → Spin.
