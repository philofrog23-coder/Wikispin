# WikiSpin privacy policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

WikiSpin does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

**What stays on your device.** Your saved articles, spin history, streak and settings are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Requests to Wikipedia.** To show an article, the app requests its summary from Wikipedia's public API (en.wikipedia.org). These requests contain the article title and the app's name and version, but no identifier for you. When you tap Read, the article opens in an in-app browser on Wikipedia. Those requests are handled by the Wikimedia Foundation under its own privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

**Sharing.** When you share an article, the image and link go only to the app or person you choose in the system share sheet.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
